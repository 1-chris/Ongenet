# Timeline & clips

## Quick steps

1. Add tracks from the **track header menu**; arm for recording, mute/solo from the header or mixer.
2. Drag audio from **Library → Samples** onto a track; enable **Auto-stretch** in Settings if you want tempo sync on import.
3. Double-click a **MIDI clip** to open the **Piano roll** (bottom panel).
4. Right-click clips for render, freeze, linked copies, stem separation, and audio-to-MIDI.

## Details

The arrange view is the centre of the window — a scrolling, zoomable grid with bar ruler, track headers, and a moving playhead.

### Recording & editing

- **Arm** a track, press **Record** on the transport — clips grow live on the timeline.
- **Edit / Select / Slice** tools, rubber-band multi-select, duplicate, reverse, and slice from the timeline toolbar.
- **Automation lanes** — right-click a control → **Create automation track**; lanes appear under the track.

### Comping

Loop-record into **take lanes**, pick the best regions, then **flatten** the comp.

### Ripple edit

- **Ctrl+Shift+I** — insert a bar at the playhead
- **Ctrl+Shift+Backspace** — delete a bar and close the gap

### Render clip to new track

Right-click any MIDI clip, audio clip, or group summary → **Render clip to new track** to bake the full FX chain into a new audio clip below.
