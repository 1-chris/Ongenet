# Session & patterns

## Quick steps

1. Switch to the **Session** tab for clip slots per track/scene — launch clips while the transport runs.
2. Use **Channel Rack** + **Pattern editor** for FL-style step grids.
3. Place **pattern clips** on the timeline — they expand into MIDI at playback via the pattern scheduler.
4. Open **View → Section Playlist** to play arrangement markers in song-structure order.

## Details

Hybrid workflows combine timeline arrangement with launcher-style playback:

- **Session tab** — clip launcher grid aligned to tracks and scenes; scene columns launch every track at once.
- **Launch modes** — Trigger, Gate, Toggle, Repeat; launch quantize snaps starts to the beat grid.
- **Hybrid mode** — plays the timeline arrangement while session clips overdub on top.
- **Channel Rack** — overview of pattern channels with per-channel mute and pattern selection.
- **Step sequencer** — edit steps per channel; change pattern length and step count in the pattern toolbar.

Patterns are shared objects — one pattern can drive multiple instrument tracks through channel routing.
